Privacy policy

What we keep, and why.

Last updated October 13, 2026. Tripframe is an early-access product. It does not book photography sessions yet.

What you send us

If you join as a traveler, we store your name, email, destination, and the photography service you chose.

If you join as a photographer, we store your name, email, location, specialization, and portfolio or Instagram link if you include one.

We also keep the time the request was saved. We do not ask for a password, payment card, or precise GPS location.

Why we store it

We use these details to contact you about early access and to understand which cities and services people want. We do not sell your information, and we do not pass it to an advertising network.

Where it lives

Form submissions are saved in Tripframe’s Netlify Database, a private PostgreSQL database. The site does not put database credentials in the browser. A Netlify Function checks each submission before anything is stored.

How long we keep it

We keep lead records until you ask us to delete them, or until we no longer need them to launch Tripframe.

Your choices

Email hello@tripframe.com to ask for a copy of your details or to delete them.
